A guide to what commands Management are permitted to use.

# **Important Information**

As a member of Management at Wingin' It you have access to a number of administrative commands used to perform essential duties, such as hosting shifts, enforcing rules, and managing training sessions. To ensure consistency and prevent misuse, this guide outlines which commands are permitted.<br /><br />Any use of unapproved commands may result in a **reminder** for the first offence and a **Hyra strike** for subsequent violations. Repeated abuse may lead to a formal investigation by the Internal Affairs Department.

# **Permitted Commands**

## **General Commands**

<Info>
  These commands may be used in any appropriate scenario.
</Info>

> :chatlogs<br />:clicklogs<br />:cmdbox<br />:joinlogs<br />:kick (In compliance with the MAA guide)<br />:kickwarn (In compliance with the MAA guide)<br />:leavelogs<br />:logs<br />:pm<br />:ref<br />:removewarning<br />:serverban (In compliance with the MAA guide)<br />:to<br />:touchlogs<br />:unview<br />:view<br />:warn (In compliance with the MAA guide)<br />:warnings

## **Shift Only Commands**

<Info>
  These are strictly to be used during hosted shift sessions.
</Info>

> :n<br />:role<br />:unrole

## **Training Only Commands**

<Info>
  These commands are to be used only during training sessions.
</Info>

> :countdown<br />:dm<br />:give (to give Trainee’s tools, where necessary)<br />:names<br />:quizstart<br />:scanitem<br />:setmsg<br />:shutdown<br />:slock<br />:team (teaming yourself Host, where necessary)<br />:whitelist add<br />:whitelist on<br />:whitelist remove

# **Summary**

Using **any command not listed** in this document while on duty in any Wingin' It game will result in disciplinary action:

- **1st Offence** - Reminder issued
- **Further Offences** - Hyra strike(s) issued

Continued misuse or command abuse may trigger a full investigation by the Internal Affairs department. If you believe any commands have been missed, please contact our Departmental Director. For any questions about the guide, contact a member of the Corporate team.
