# **Hannah Evanns - Executive Director**

> **Specialises in:** Studio leadership, legal oversight, and overall business operations.

**Responsibilities:**

- Handle all DMCA claims, requests, and legal inquiries.
- Seek and coordinate legal advice when required.
- Oversee and coordinate all game development and ensure stability across all Wingin' It projects.
- Plan and approve blueprints for new games.
- Supervise all departments, rules, guidelines, and staff activity (executive, corporate, and management levels).
- Ensure all management members meet performance standards.
- Manage websites, external servers, and APIs.
- Work with external contractors to maintain servers.
- Oversee executive management and collaborate on group improvement.
- Manage marketing and advertising for all games and the Event Centre.
- Handle all financial management, payment decisions, and development partnerships.
- Oversee database management and ensure data integrity.
- Engage with the community and host public events.
- Ensure compliance with EU laws, Roblox ToS, and UK GDPR.

**Summary:** As the founder, Cuba oversees every aspect of Wingin' It, from legal compliance to game development. He ensures the studio runs smoothly, management meets expectations, and all projects maintain a high professional standard.
