  All management **must** follow the guide below when taking moderation actions. If you see a member of management failing to follow this guide, please open a report ticket within the communities Discord server.
</Info>

# **Discord Server Moderation**

### **Disrespectful behaviour towards other members of the server:**

- Verbal warning, and a further Dyno warning each time it occurs.

### **Persistent spamming in chats:**

- 1-hour timeout, kick from server, then ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Sharing inappropriate speech/content (NSFW):**

- Verbal warning, and a further Dyno warning, then ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Discrimination based on race, religion, gender or sexuality:**

- Verbal warning, and a further Dyno warning, then ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Impersonation of Discord staff:**

- Report to Discord Moderation team.

### **Disrupting official server events or activities:**

- Dyno warning, and a further temporary ban from events or activities. Consult with Executive Management in any further situations. 

### **Threatening or encouraging harm to others:**

- Verbal warning, and a further Dyno warning each time it occurs.

### **Intentionally provoking/arguing with others:**

- Verbal warning, and a further Dyno warning each time it occurs.

### **Advertising other Discord servers or unrelated websites without Executive approval:**

- Verbal warning, then ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Sharing others’ personal information without consent: (Location, Email Address, Full Names, etc.):**

- Immediate ban from the server.

### **Posting or sharing leaked or rank-locked content: **

- Immediate ban from the server.

### **Posting or sharing links to malicious or harmful content: **

- Immediate ban from the server.

### **Encouraging or engaging in illegal activity(s): **

- Immediate ban from the server.

### **Excessive use of profanity towards others (with friendly banter as an exception): **

- Verbal warning, then a further verbal warning. Dyno warning each time it occurs if the user continues.

### **Using modifications against Discord Terms of Use: **

- Report to Discord Moderation team.

### **Disrupting voice channels with excessive noise: **

- Verbal warning, then a further verbal warning. Dyno warning if the user continues, and consult with Executive Management in any further situations. 

### **Not meeting Discord’s minimum age requirement (13 years old): **

- Immediate ban from the server, appealable when the user turns 13, and report to Discord Moderation team.

### **Intentionally creating unnecessary drama with others: **

- Verbal warning, then a further verbal warning. Dyno warning each time it occurs if the user continues.

### **Bypassing auto-mod rules: **

- Dyno warning each time.

### **Evading moderation actions by using alternate account(s): **

- Repeat the action taken on the previous account(s).

# **Roblox Experience Moderation**

### **Bullying or Harassment:**

1. Warning and a reminder about respecting others.
2. 15-minute mute.
3. Server-only ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Hacking or Exploiting:**

1. Immediate permanent ban if confirmed with evidence.

### **Wearing Inappropriate Content (Avatar Items):**

1. Report to Roblox Moderation team and request the removal of content.
2. Warning, followed by a server-only ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Discrimination based on race, religion, gender or sexuality:**

1. Warning and a reminder about respecting others.
2. 30-minute mute.
3. Server-only ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Inappropriate Speech (NSFW):**

1. Report to Roblox and a reminder on appropriate conduct.
2. 30-minute mute.
3. Server-only ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Spamming in Chat:**

1. Warning and report to Roblox.
2. 15-minute mute.
3. Server-only ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Disruptive Behaviour in Voice Chat:**

1. Warning, and server-only ban if the behaviour persists. _Note: Roblox should take action automatically._

### **Impersonation of Roblox Staff:**

1. Immediate server-only ban and report to Roblox Moderation team.

### **Sharing Personal Information (Location / Address, Full Names, etc.)**

1. Warning and a reminder about privacy and safety.
2. Report to Roblox Moderation team.

### **Sharing Leaked or Rank-Locked Content: **

1. Server-only ban for sharing prohibited content.
2. Permanent ban if the individual returns and persists with the behaviour.

### **Disrupting Official Group Events or Tournaments: **

1. Warning and a reminder about event etiquette.
2. Temporary ban from participating in events.
3. Server-only ban if the behaviour persists.

### **Encouraging The Use or Distribution of Cheating Tools (Exploits)**

1. Server-only ban for encouraging the use of or distributing cheating tools, with evidence, and report to Roblox Moderation team.

### **Bypassing Chat Filter:**

1. Warning and a reminder about following the rules.
2. Server-only ban if the behaviour persists.

### <br />**Evading Moderation by Using Alternate Account(s):**

1. Repeat the action taken on the previous account(s).

### **Encouraging or Supporting Extremist Situations (Terrorism, Shootings, etc.):**

1. Immediate server-only ban, and a permanent ban if the individual returns and persists with the behaviour.
2. Report to the Roblox Moderation team.

### **Violating Roblox Terms of Services:**

1. Report to the Roblox Moderation team.

### **Intentionally exploiting game or Roblox bugs:**

1. Warning, followed by a server-only ban if the behaviour persists.
