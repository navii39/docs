Here you’ll find instructions on how to appeal any type of ban for Wingin’ It Restaurants.

# **Appealing Discord Bans**

To appeal a **Wingin’ It Restaurants** Discord ban, please use this form: [**https://links.winginit.uk/discordappeals**](https://links.winginit.uk/discordappeals)

<Warning>
  Please have your **DMs open** to allow Dyno to respond whenever our team have made a verdict in regards to your appeal.
</Warning>

# **Appealing Roblox Game Bans**

To appeal a **Wingin’ It Restaurants Game Ban,** please use this game: [**https://links.winginit.uk/robloxappeals**](https://links.winginit.uk/robloxappeals)

<Warning>
  Please note that we use a centralised ban database meaning if you receive a permanent ban from one of our games, **you will be banned from** **all of our games.**
</Warning>
